The Sandigan City DRRMO is the frontline agency in safeguarding the city from disasters and emergencies. With a centralized, civilian-run system, it implements comprehensive disaster risk reduction strategies and coordinates swift response efforts through its elite Sandigan Emergency Response Team (SERT).

2. Legal Basis

  • Republic Act No. 10121 – The Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010
  • Sandigan City Ordinance No. 2025-001 – Establishing SC DRRMO as a permanent LGU department
  • DILG Memorandum Circular 2012-73 – Guidelines on the organization of local DRRM offices

3. Mission and Vision

Mission:
To build a disaster-resilient Sandigan City by promoting a culture of preparedness, enabling rapid and effective response, and ensuring swift recovery through collaborative governance and modern solutions.

Vision:
A safe, adaptive, and resilient Sandigan City, where every citizen is empowered and protected against disasters and emergencies.

6. Emergency Guidelines

Before:

  • Prepare Go-Bags with essential supplies
  • Know your barangay evacuation site
  • Monitor official alerts from SC DRRMO

During:

  • Stay calm and follow official advisories
  • Evacuate immediately when instructed
  • Prioritize life over belongings

After:

  • Report any injuries or hazards
  • Wait for clearance before returning home
  • Coordinate with your barangay DRRM for assistance
